Salon Marketplace Platform
Role-Wise & Page-Wise Feature Map — Every Screen, Every Feature
How to Read This Document
Every screen/page/tab across all 3 deliverables (Customer App, Partner App, SuperAdmin Website) is listed here under the role that uses it, in the order a user would typically encounter them. Every feature discussed across the Feature Specification and its Addendum is placed on the exact page it belongs to. Pages that didn't exist in the original scope and were added to support the newer features are marked NEW PAGE.

A. Customer App
1  Login / Signup
Phone number based signup/login with OTP
Basic profile details — name, gender,dob,address
2  Home / Discover
Search bar for services or salons
Promotional banner slideshow (controlled by SuperAdmin)
Gender toggle to tailor shown services
Browse by service category
"Book Again" section — past-purchased services from previously visited salons
Salons with an expired subscription appear greyed out / marked unavailable rather than fully hidden
3  Salon Profile Page
Salon photos
Full service list with price, duration, description
Tapping a service adds it directly to the cart
Sticky bottom bar with running total and "View Cart" button
If the salon's subscription has expired: page shows a clear "temporarily unavailable" state, booking disabled
4  QR Scanner   NEW PAGE
Scan icon at the top of the Home screen
Opens device camera to scan a salon's printed QR code
On successful scan, navigates directly to that salon's profile page
5  Cart
Lists all services/combos added from a salon
Suggested add-on services from the same salon (upsell)
6  Book Appointment
Select one or more services, or a combo
Service Provider selection is compulsory — includes an explicit "Any Available" option, auto-resolved at booking time
Quick date options (Today, Tomorrow, This Saturday, This Sunday) plus a 30-minute-block time picker
Unavailable slots (staff busy, outside working hours, on leave, outside salon's general hours) greyed out automatically
Real-time availability check via server-side calculation against staff hours, breaks, leave, and existing bookings
Suggests next available slot / alternate staff member if the chosen one is unavailable
Shows total charges and the advance amount payable now
Payment gateway checkout for the advance amount
Appointment confirmed and slot locked on successful payment
7  My Bookings (Upcoming / Past tabs)
View upcoming appointments — date, time, staff, services, advance paid, balance remaining
View past appointment history
Cancel — allowed only up to the cutoff set by SuperAdmin (starting value: 90 minutes before appointment start)
Refund on cancellation follows the specific service/combo's refund setting
Reschedule, subject to the same cutoff and availability check
If a customer cancels/reschedules beyond the same-day abuse threshold, further same-day bookings require full upfront payment (advance-only no longer applies for that day)
Rescheduling prompt with a free-of-cost reschedule link if the salon announces a day closure affecting a booked appointment
8  Generate Appointment QR   NEW PAGE
Replaces the old OTP verification screen entirely
"Generate QR" button available any time during the day, for today's appointment only
Displays a unique QR code for staff to scan at the salon
QR validity duration is controlled by SuperAdmin (Platform Policy Settings) and never valid past the appointment date
9  Payments & Receipts
Pay remaining balance at the salon (cash/card/UPI) or in-app
Digital receipt/invoice — service breakdown, advance paid, balance paid
Same receipt also delivered automatically via WhatsApp
10  Notifications
Booking confirmation, appointment reminders, cancellation confirmation
Day-closure / mass-reschedule alerts
Delivered via in-app notification and WhatsApp
11  Support Chatbot   NEW PAGE
Rule-based FAQ assistant — appointment status, how to cancel/reschedule, payment/receipt questions, how booking works
Escalates unresolved queries to human support
12  Profile / Settings
Personal details, gender
View saved payment methods (if applicable)
B. Partner App — Service Provider
Service Provider is one of three roles inside the single Partner App — the screens below are shown only to users logged in with this role.
1  Login
Role-based login — app shows Service Provider screens automatically
2  Daily / Weekly Schedule
View appointments assigned to them, filterable by date
Appointment details — customer name, services, time, advance paid, balance due, phone number
3  Add Offline / Walk-In Appointment
Add a walk-in customer's appointment directly, same real-time availability check as the Customer App
Service Provider selection compulsory, same as customer-side booking
4  Scan Customer QR — Start Session   NEW PAGE
Scans the customer's appointment QR to begin the session
If a Service Provider other than the originally booked one scans, the system treats them as the one now serving this appointment
Confirmation prompt before starting: "Starting session for [Provider Name] — confirm?"
Appointment status updates to In Progress on confirmation
5  Appointment Status & Billing
Update status: Scheduled → In Progress → Completed, or mark No-show
Adjust the final billed amount if extra work was done on the spot
6  Add Service Mid-Appointment (Dynamic Bill Adjustment)   NEW PAGE
Add an extra service to an in-progress appointment, no digital customer approval required
Select which Service Provider is delivering this specific added service (may differ from the main provider)
Bill automatically recalculates, itemized to show original vs. added services separately
7  Leave Management
Request leave — full day or specific hours, on a specific date
Select leave type: Paid or Unpaid
Leave is auto-approved or requires Admin's approval, depending on how Admin has configured it for this specific staff member
View leave request history and status
8  My Salary & Earnings   NEW PAGE
View base salary, commission earned this month, unpaid-leave deductions
View total payable amount for the current and past months
View Paid / Unpaid status for each month's salary
9  Profile
Personal details and specialization (limited edit — Admin manages the master record)
View own working hours, breaks/lunch break, and weekly off (set by Admin, capped within the salon's general hours)
C. Partner App — Admin
Admin is a second role inside the same Partner App, showing a different, more expansive set of screens.
1  Login / Salon Switcher   NEW PAGE
One Admin account can own and manage multiple salons
Salon switcher at the top of the app — all screens below scope to the currently selected salon
2  Register New Salon
Submit a new salon (name, address, photos, working hours, contact details)
Submission enters Pending status and appears in SuperAdmin's Approval Queue
Track approval status; edit and resubmit if rejected
3  Global Appointments Dashboard
View all appointments across all staff, filterable by date, staff, service, and status
Daily workload view per staff member
4  Scan Customer QR — Start Session   NEW PAGE
Same QR-scan flow as Service Provider — Admin selects which staff member is attending, then confirms before the session starts
5  Add Service Mid-Appointment (Dynamic Bill Adjustment)   NEW PAGE
Same capability as Service Provider — add and assign extra services to an in-progress appointment
6  Staff Management
Add a new staff member — personal details, specialization
Set working hours, weekly off, breaks/lunch (excluded from bookable slots automatically); shifts cannot be set outside the salon's own general working hours
Set base salary and commission percentage for the staff member (editable anytime)
Set whether this staff member's leave requests are auto-approved or need manual approval
Edit or deactivate a staff member
Approve/reject leave requests (for staff not on auto-approve) and view the full leave calendar
7  Service Management
Add a service by picking from SuperAdmin's master catalog (name + estimated time pre-filled) and entering only the price
Or create a fully custom service/category not on the master list — visible only to this salon; SuperAdmin is notified
Create and edit combo services
Assign which staff members can perform which services
8  Salon Settings   NEW PAGE
Set the salon's general working hours (acts as the outer cap on every staff member's shift)
Mark specific dates as Salon Closed
View advance-payment defaults set by SuperAdmin for this salon (required/refundable/percentage) — can still be overridden at individual service level
9  Day Cancellation & Mass Reschedule
Cancel all appointments for a day in one action during an emergency closure
Automatically notifies affected customers with a free reschedule link via WhatsApp; advance carried forward, not forfeited
10  Revenue & Business Analytics   NEW PAGE
Today's, last 7-day, last 30-day, and custom-range revenue
Cash vs. online revenue split
Online-booked vs. walk-in revenue split
Revenue and appointment count per staff member
Average bill value, repeat vs. new customer ratio, top services, busiest hours
Toggle between single-salon and combined view across all owned salons
Export any report to Excel via the device's share sheet
11  Advance Payment Dashboard
View total advance collected for the salon
View how much has been distributed by SuperAdmin and how much is currently pending
12  Payroll Management   NEW PAGE
View each staff member's monthly payable amount — base salary, commission earned, unpaid-leave deductions
Mark a staff member's monthly salary as Paid or Unpaid
View payroll history by month
13  Subscription & Billing   NEW PAGE
View current plan (Starter / Growth / Commission Plan) and expiry date
In-app expiry warning banner, shown starting 3 days before expiry
Renew or upgrade the subscription plan
14  Wallet   NEW PAGE
View current coin balance
Coin-earning history (which milestone triggered which reward)
Redeem coins toward next subscription purchase or against commission owed
15  Complaints
View complaints raised by customers against the salon, and their resolution status
D. Partner App — Collaborator
Collaborator is the third role inside the Partner App, scoped narrowly to on-ground salon onboarding.
1  Login
Role-based login — app shows Collaborator screens automatically
2  Onboard New Salon
Create a new salon profile — name, address, photos, contact info, working hours
Optionally add services and pricing (from master catalog or custom, same as Admin) — not compulsory
Submit for SuperAdmin approval
Draft saved locally on the device if offline (patchy field signal), synced automatically once back online
3  My Onboarded Salons
List of all salons onboarded by this Collaborator, with status: Pending / Approved / Rejected
Edit access to a salon is permanently removed once SuperAdmin approves it
4  Assigned Salons & Alerts   NEW PAGE
Salons assigned to this Collaborator by SuperAdmin (via the landing-page enquiry form)
Receives an alert when an assigned salon's subscription is about to expire
5  Profile
View and manage personal profile and contact details
E. SuperAdmin Portal (Website)
1  Dashboard
Platform-wide overview — active salons, bookings, revenue snapshot
2  Salon Approval Queue
List of salons awaiting review — self-registered or submitted by a Collaborator
Review full submitted details
Approve (salon goes live, QR auto-generated) or Reject with a reason sent back to the submitter
3  Salon Directory
Master list of every salon, filterable by status, searchable by name/city
View a salon's full profile — owner info, services, staff, appointment volume, revenue, complaint history
Suspend, reactivate, or permanently deactivate a salon
4  Salon Emergency Edit   NEW PAGE
Directly edit any salon's services, prices, and details without involving that salon's Admin
Not surfaced to the salon when used; every action is still recorded in the internal Audit Log
5  Service Category & Master Catalog   NEW PAGE
Maintain the master list of service categories
Maintain the master catalog of services — name and estimated duration (no price at this level)
Review notifications when a salon adds a custom category/service
Promote a salon's custom service to the master catalog — becomes available to all salons; estimated duration locked at the value set during promotion
6  Platform Policy Settings   NEW PAGE
QR session validity duration
Cancellation/reschedule cutoff (starting default: 90 minutes)
Same-day cancellation/reschedule abuse threshold and penalty rule
Subscription expiry warning window (starting default: 3 days)
All values editable without any code changes
7  Per-Salon Payment Settings   NEW PAGE
Set advance-required (yes/no), refundable (yes/no), and advance percentage independently for each salon
Soft default — the salon's own Admin can still override at the individual service level
8  Banners
Create, schedule, and target promotional banners — platform-wide, city-specific, or salon-specific
9  Platform-Wide Reporting
Total active salons, total bookings (today/week/month), average rating, top-performing salons
Platform-wide and per-salon cancellation/no-show rates
Export any report to Excel
10  Payment Collection & Distribution
View total advance collected, filterable by salon and date range
"Mark as Distributed" action, with a full distribution history log
Dynamic weekly (Wednesday-based) payout calculation, ready for manual confirmation — not automatic
For salons on the Commission Plan: commission automatically deducted from the payout before distribution, shown as a separate line on the payout record
11  Subscription Plan Management   NEW PAGE
Edit Starter and Growth plan details — price, monthly WhatsApp campaign limit, and full feature list
Manage Commission Plan assignments — set a salon's commission percentage and choose whether it receives Starter- or Growth-level features
12  Wallet Scheme Management   NEW PAGE
Set the value of one coin (₹)
Create and edit reward ladder schemes (e.g., tiered coin rewards per completed-appointment milestone)
Run multiple schemes simultaneously
13  Enquiry / Lead Management   NEW PAGE
View salon enquiries submitted through the public landing website form
Assign each enquiry to a specific Collaborator for on-ground onboarding
14  Collaborator Management   NEW PAGE
View all Collaborators, salons they've onboarded, and basic performance stats
Reassign a salon to a different Collaborator if needed
15  Complaints & Disputes
View and manage complaints raised against any salon — mark under review, resolved, or dismissed
16  Audit Log   NEW PAGE
Internal record of sensitive SuperAdmin actions (e.g., emergency salon edits) — actor, action, timestamp
17  Public Landing Website — Enquiry Form   NEW PAGE
Public-facing page (not behind login) where a prospective salon owner submits interest in joining the platform
Feeds directly into the Enquiry / Lead Management screen above

as the primary double-booking mechanism.

appointed_provider_id = same provider

appointment time range overlaps

status IN ('confirmed', 'in_progress')

10:00–11:30
10:30–11:00
being simultaneously booked.

37. Most important database rule
I want this explicitly added to the document immediately after the schema:
Appointment Concurrency Rule
The appointments table must not rely on a simple unique (appointed_provider_id, appointment_date, start_time) constraint for double-booking protection. PostgreSQL range/exclusion constraints must prevent overlapping appointment intervals for the same provider. Application-level availability validation remains necessary for business rules, but the database constraint is the final concurrency safeguard.
This is especially important because the existing document currently describes the simple unique constraint as the database-level guarantee.

38. Financial source-of-truth rule
Add this immediately after the payment tables:
appointments.total_amount, advance_amount, balance_amount and final_billed_amount are operational snapshots for fast display and workflow decisions. They must not be treated as the authoritative financial ledger. Successful payments, refunds, payment allocations, payouts and wallet transactions are immutable financial records and must be used for reconciliation and reporting.
This is the most important conceptual change to the financial architecture.

39. Rescheduling rule
Add:
Rescheduling must not destroy the financial history of the original appointment. Successful payment records remain associated with their original transaction, while payment_allocations may carry the paid amount forward to the new appointment. A free reschedule caused by salon closure must preserve the customer's paid amount without generating an artificial refund.
This directly fixes the rescheduling problem in the old schema.

40. Combo rule
Add:
When a combo is booked, the appointment must snapshot its constituent services into appointment_services. combo_id identifies the originating combo, while service_id identifies the actual service line. This allows service-level provider assignment, duration tracking, payroll commission calculation and historical billing even if the combo definition later changes.
This is why I changed appointment_services from "service OR combo" to service always + optional combo origin.

41. QR security rule
Add:
The raw appointment QR token must never be stored in the database. Generate a cryptographically random short-lived token, return it to the Customer App, store only its SHA-256 hash, and invalidate the previous token whenever a new QR is generated. QR verification must occur inside a transaction and must prevent a second successful scan of an already-started appointment.
The current document correctly requires short-lived appointment QR codes, but the database should not store the raw credential

Architect's Design Notes
Catalog vs. Instance layering (service_templates vs. services)
Separating what a service is (name, category, estimated duration — service_templates) from what a salon charges for it (services) is what makes the master-catalog + salon-custom-service system in Section 7 of the addendum work cleanly. A salon's custom service is just a template row scoped to that salon; "promoting" it to standard is a single field update (promoted_to_standard_at), not a data migration.
appointed_provider_id vs. serving_provider_id
Splitting these two fields is what makes both the anti-double-booking guarantee and accurate commission tracking possible at the same time. The slot lock and unique constraint apply to appointed_provider_id (reserved at booking time); serving_provider_id is only set once, at QR scan confirmation, and is what payroll calculations (Module 6) actually key off.
Snapshot tables (salary_payouts, salon_payouts, appointment_services)
None of these are computed live from current rates. A provider's commission % can change next month without altering what they were owed last month, because salary_payouts stores a snapshot at calculation time. Same logic protects salon_payouts and appointment_services against retroactive drift if prices or commission percentages change later.
Generic platform_policy_settings and audit_log
Both are intentionally generic (key-value, and entity-agnostic respectively) rather than growing a dedicated table or column per new configurable value or auditable action. This keeps future policy additions (e.g., a new configurable threshold) or new sensitive actions worth logging a one-row insert, not a schema migration.
Why wallet tracks a running appointment counter
salon_wallets.completed_online_appointments_count exists so ladder-tier evaluation (Module 8) doesn't require a COUNT() over the entire appointments history every time a booking completes — the counter increments once per completion and is checked against each active scheme's tiers directly.
Why device tracking and version control are app_type + platform aware
Both user_devices and app_versions key off (app_type, platform) rather than a single global row, because the Customer App and Partner App will realistically ship updates on different schedules, and Android/iOS release cycles rarely line up (iOS review takes longer). This lets SuperAdmin force an update on, say, Partner App Android only — without accidentally blocking Customer App users on iOS who don't need that particular fix.
