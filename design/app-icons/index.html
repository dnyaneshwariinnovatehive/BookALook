BookALook — "B" lettermark icons

16 lettermarks built only from B or b, the way the best-known brands work (Facebook's f, Booking.com's B., Beats' b, Pinterest's P). Each card shows the Customer and Partner apps as a pair: the same letter, told apart by colour. Use the toggles to restyle every icon at once, and click a card to see it on a home screen.

Home-screen preview

Icons are drawn full-bleed on a 100×100 grid, the way the App Store and Play Store want them. iOS and Android apply their own corner masks.
